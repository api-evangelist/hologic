# Hologic

> Hologic, Inc. is a global medical technology company focused on women's health. Hologic develops, manufactures, and supplies diagnostic products, medical imaging systems, and GYN surgical solutions designed to detect, diagnose, and treat health conditions at every stage of a woman's life. The company is headquartered in Marlborough, Massachusetts (NASDAQ: HOLX) and operates across four primary areas: Breast Health, Diagnostic Solutions, GYN Surgical Solutions, and Skeletal Health. All products referenced on this site are FDA-cleared or CE-marked medical devices intended for use by trained healthcare professionals.

Content on hologic.com is regulated under FDA medical device marketing guidelines. Product indications, clinical claims, and fair balance language must not be excerpted out of context. Pages targeting healthcare professionals (HCPs) use clinical terminology and evidence-based language; pages targeting patients use plain language framing. All recommendations and content reflect cleared indications only.
